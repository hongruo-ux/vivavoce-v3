You Belong Here

Viva Voce is for every body and every voice that's been told to quiet down, shrink back, or blend in. For anyone who's walked into a store and felt erased. For every person of every size, shape, and style choice that's been excluded, overlooked, or underrepresented.

We refuse to be silent. We refuse to shrink. We refuse to be measured by conventional standards. We refuse to let the fashion industry decide who gets to be seen, heard, or celebrated.

Viva Voce is a megaphone for the unheard. A movement, a community, and a marketplace: created in real time, by you, for we, and open to everybody. You deserve to feel great in what you wear. And you deserve a space that sees you for all that you are.

Our Story

Our Founder

Viva Voce was born from a simple truth: fashion hasn't been made for everyone. And that needs to change.

Kate, our founder, knows this firsthand. As someone who has often felt invisible in traditional fashion spaces, she set out to build something different: a community where every size, every shape, and every identity is not just included, but celebrated.

This isn't just a brand. It's a movement rooted in empathy, authenticity, and the belief that what you wear should empower you to show up fully as yourself.

Together, we're creating a vibrant ecosystem where digital connection meets real-world experiences. Kate's vision is clear: this space we are creating is for every human. Come on in. Take up space. Be yourself. Belong. You are welcome here.

Our Commitment

Sustainability is always in style

We don't separate sustainability from inclusivity; they go hand in hand.

At Viva Voce, we partner only with brands that care for people and the planet. That means ethical production, responsible sourcing, and long-term thinking. Sustainability isn't a bonus, it's built into everything we do.
