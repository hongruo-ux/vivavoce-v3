02 — Role Hierarchy

Every font has a job.

GT Alpina Fine Extended Regular → Headline
You belong here.

Used large and sparingly for maximum emotional impact. Italic variant available for warmth and expressiveness. Never use for body text — it's a statement maker, not a workhorse.

Sharp Earth Medium, All Caps, +5% tracking → Subline
Visible. Valid. Valued.  ·  Brand Strategy  ·  Our Voice

Section openers, labels, and callouts. All caps is mandatory. The 5% letter-spacing creates breathing room and authority. Never lowercase in this role.

Sharp Earth Regular → Body / Copy
There are two distinct sides to our personality – that's what makes us powerful. Our warmth makes us even more effective as warriors. We are here to create a safe space, that is open to everybody.

Comfortable reading weight (Light / Regular). Generous line-height (1.65–1.75). Used for all paragraphs, descriptions, and UI copy.

05 — Do / Don't

Rules to live by.

✕ Don't

Use serif for body text

GT Alpina is headlines-only. Using it at small sizes hurts legibility and dilutes its emotional impact.

✓ Do

Big, bold, rare.

Deploy the headline font sparingly — it should feel like a reveal every time it appears on the page.

✕ Don't

subline in lowercase

Sublines must always be all-caps with ~5% letter-spacing (0.05em). Lowercase removes the structural authority.

✓ Do

SUBLINE IN ALL CAPS

Consistent all-caps + tracking creates the confident, structured feel that anchors sections.

✕ Don't

Mix third fonts, decorative faces, or platform defaults (Arial, system-ui, San Francisco) in external communications.

✓ Do

Use Montagu Slab + Roboto exclusively for internal/Google contexts (slides, docs). All external brand touchpoints must use GT Alpina + Sharp Earth.

✕ Don't

Use the heavy headline font over cluttered backgrounds or at small sizes where the humanist details get lost.

✓ Do

Give headlines breathing room. The brand guide explicitly calls for "plenty of space" — typography needs air, just like the people it celebrates.

Google Font Alternates — Internal Use Only

When GT Alpina and Sharp Earth aren't available (Google Slides, Google Docs, internal decks), use Montagu Slab in place of GT Alpina Extended, and Roboto in place of Sharp Earth. The same hierarchy rules apply. These alternates are strictly for internal communications — never use them on external brand collateral, website, ads, or packaging.
